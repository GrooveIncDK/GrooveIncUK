Proof, not promises. Every tier and extension on the Solutions page is already doing real work for a real business — including Groove Inc's own.

Omega Essentials
Fixed price · Accessible entry

Yo Yah

A yoga and fitness brand that needed real recognisability without a full production system — the entry point the Essentials tier exists for. Logo construction, clear-space geometry, a primary colour system and a typographic hierarchy, nothing more, nothing less.

The same engagement set the template for Yo Yah's own photography rules — black-and-white for yoga, warm duotone for fitness, no stock-photo close-ups — which now anchor Groove Inc's Compliance & Content Retainer example.

Omega Environment
Retail · Physical space

Hindsholm Gårdbutik

A working farm shop on Hindsholm, Denmark, selling cherries, redcurrants and blackcurrants from a self-serve roadside stand. The Environment tier took the brand off the screen entirely — interior fittings, exterior signage, product labelling — with every colour re-specified against whatever standard actually governs that surface: RAL for paint, PMS for printed labels, CMYK for anything litho.

This is the proof point for the claim that an Omega Document survives contact with a physical shopfront, not just a screen.

Brand Architecture
Extension · Multi-entity

Stålbæk Holding

Four related entities under one parent — Restaurant, Beef, Gårdbutik and Produktion — each needing its own mark while staying visibly part of the same family. Brand Architecture repeats and cross-references the full document across all four, so the colour and type system stays consistent everywhere the sub-brands diverge.
