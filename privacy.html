Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Last updated 16 September 2026

1. Who we are

Staalbaek IT Dev ApS, trading as Groove Inc, CVR 41564571, Fynshovedvej 584, 5390 Martofte, Denmark, is the data controller for this website (grooveinc.uk) under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What information we actually collect

This site does not use a contact form, analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies of any kind. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us directly — by emailing hello@grooveinc.uk or admin@grooveinc.dk, calling +45 3115 5327, or otherwise reaching out. That typically means your name, contact details, and whatever you tell us about your business or project.

3. Google Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request. We don't control this data flow beyond choosing to use Google Fonts; Google's own privacy policy describes how it's handled on their end.

4. Why we process this information

We process the information you send us to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to deliver and invoice an engagement. The legal basis is either taking steps at your request prior to entering a contract, performance of a contract with you, or our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries sent to us directly.

5. How long we keep it

If we don't end up working together, we keep enquiry correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to conclude that conversation. If an engagement goes ahead, related records are kept for as long as our accounting and tax obligations require, including our UK VAT record-keeping obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion of it, restrict or object to our processing, and request a copy in a portable format. You can exercise any of these by contacting us at hello@grooveinc.uk. If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. International transfers

Staalbaek IT Dev ApS is based in Denmark, within the European Economic Area; the UK government's data protection adequacy regulations recognise the EEA as providing an adequate level of protection, so this transfer doesn't require additional safeguards. Loading Google Fonts separately involves a transfer of your IP address to Google, which may process data outside the UK/EEA; Google participates in recognised transfer mechanisms intended to provide an adequate level of protection for such transfers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always the one published here, with the date it was last updated shown above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@grooveinc.uk, Staalbaek IT Dev ApS, Fynshovedvej 584, 5390 Martofte, Denmark.

This page describes our actual data practices as accurately as we can and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend independent legal review before relying on it as a complete UK GDPR compliance statement, particularly if our data practices change (e.g. if we add analytics, cookies, or a contact form in future).
